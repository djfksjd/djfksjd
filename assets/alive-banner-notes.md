Artwork: built-in image generation tool. Animation: 15-second particle assembly and dissolve loop, 1100 × 400 GIF. The wordmark stays still while the creature changes.

## Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: cinematic creature artwork source for an animated ALIVE GitHub brand banner.
Create ONE wide landscape image, aspect ratio 3:2, composed as a precise 2 by 2 contact sheet of four independent creature portraits on identical pure black backgrounds. No gutters, no lines, no writing. Each creature centered in its own quadrant with generous black margin all around, no creature crosses the quadrant boundary. Top-left: a beautiful living Korean black-and-white magpie in flight, wings spread, feathers anatomically detailed. Top-right: a majestic living owl facing camera, amber eyes, wings partly opened. Bottom-left: a living grey wolf in side profile, head raised slightly, entire torso and legs visible. Bottom-right: an elegant slender living thread-waisted wasp in macro profile with translucent wings and long fine legs.
Style: exceptionally refined cinematic natural-history art, anatomically realistic animals with detailed feather, fur and wing textures, as if they are being created from drifting stardust. Subtle warm champagne light traces the animal contours, tiny gold and cool silver particles trail from body edges into black. Bodies should be mostly photoreal and coherent, only perimeter breaks into particles. Dramatic fine rim lighting, restrained pale gold and ivory, no green, no rainbow, no futuristic HUD, no diagrams, no plastic figurines, no typography. Godlike act of giving life in a dark void, quiet grandeur, luxury brand quality. Important: pitch-black empty background around each isolated subject, balanced four equally sized quadrants, and each animal fully inside its quadrant.
